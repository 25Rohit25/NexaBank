# Account Fees

Policy ID: NEXA-POL-FEE-001  
Effective date: 2026-09-01  
Applies to: Nexa Bank demonstration environment

## Savings account

- Monthly account fee: ₹0.
- Minimum opening deposit: ₹0 in the demonstration environment.
- Replacement physical debit card: ₹250.
- Electronic statements: free.

## Current account

- Monthly account fee: ₹199.
- The monthly fee is waived when the average monthly balance is at least ₹25,000.
- Electronic statements: free.

## Transfer fees

Transfers between accounts owned by the same Nexa customer are free. International-transfer pricing is defined only in `international-transfer-policy.md`. Taxes and third-party charges are not included unless a policy explicitly says otherwise.

> These figures are fictional portfolio-demo policy data, not real financial advice or a public offer.
